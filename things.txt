Here is a structured review of your Ampharos package based on the full source:

1. Algorithmic Speed Improvements
A. Vectorize the E-step and M-step
The biggest bottleneck is in R/matrix_utils.R:91-105. mv_e_step_log_density uses a double for loop (g × n) calling mv_log_density one observation at a time. Similarly, mv_normalize_responsibilities (lines 114-122) loops over every row sequentially.

Fix: Stack the vectorized observations into a matrix X_flat (n × (r*p)) and use matrix algebra:

Compute the Mahalanobis quadratic form in bulk: tr(X_flat V⁻¹ X_flat.T @ U⁻¹) or use rowSums.
Vectorize mv_log_sum_exp across rows by computing row-wise max via matrixStats::rowMaxs and rowSums.
B. Precompute and cache Cholesky factors in the EM loop
In mv_log_density (Matrix_Base.R, lines 126-129) and mv_em_mstep, each iteration calls chol() on U and V from scratch, plus a make_spd() call with multiple jittering attempts.

Fix: Cholesky factorize once at M-step end, store L_u=chol(U) and L_v=chol(V), and reuse in the next E-step. This removes ~4 Cholesky calls per component per iteration.

C. Replace the pure-R DBSCAN with dbscan::dbscan
R/matrix_init_dbscan.R:141-183 reimplements DBSCAN manually, including a full dist() matrix and an O(n²) neighbor search loop.

Fix: Use the dbscan package's kd-tree-based implementation (dbscan::dbscan) or at least use RcppHDBSCAN for scale.

D. Reduce EM-refine init burn-in cost
R/matrix_init_emrefine.R:21 defaults to max_iter = 100. That is nearly a full fit just for initialization.

Fix: Reduce to 3-5 iterations with a warm start, or make it proportional to n/10.

E. Bootstrap script: cut n_boot or vectorize
scripts/estimate_k_ks_bootstrap_compare.R:186-223 loops over n_boot = 200 bootstrap draws. Each draw re-fits a full mgmm and scores KS. This is O(n_grid * n_boot) full fits.

Fix: Drop n_boot to 50-100 and use future.apply properly, or stratify by component. For the DBSCAN init code, mv_dbscan_heuristic_eps (lines 113-131) computes a full dist() matrix; replace with RANN for speed.

F. Avoid repeated encoding/decoding in mv_log_density
mv_log_density calls make_spd on both U and V before Cholesky, even if they were already SPD from the previous M-step. Set a flag to skip make_spd when det(U) > 0 and all(eigen(U)$values > 0).

2. Clustering / Statistical Quality Improvements
A. Regularize covariances with shrinkage
make_spd (R/Matrix_Base.R:20) only does diagonal jitter. For small n and moderate r or p, sample U and V are numerically unstable.

Fix: Add an optional Ledoit-Wolf shrinkage step on row and column covariances inside mv_em_mstep (after the sufficient statistics are collected). cov.shrink from corpcor or vanilla stick-breaking works. This stabilizes covariances, improves likelihoods, and reduces the impact of outliers on component shape.

B. Add model selection for g
You currently force g into every call. Users have no principled way to choose g.

Fix: Report BIC / AIC in summary() for a grid of g values, and suggest automated selection. For noise mixtures, use the penalized likelihood framework from McLachlan & Peel or Zhou et al. (Lagrange multiplier test for number of components).

C. Improve the KS scaling
mv_noise_ks_score (R/KS_Score.R:33) uses df = r * p for the χ² reference distribution. The theoretical combination of independent row/column Gaussian draws gives a true distribution, but in practice when U and V are estimated from the data, the deviation from χ² is systematic.

Fix: Replace the ones-sample KS test with a parametric bootstrap p-value from the null distribution under the estimated mixture. Use the same bootstrap framework in scripts/estimate_k_ks_bootstrap_compare.R but score KS as a p-value test for each k rather than just a diagnostic.

D. Handle degenerate components
In mv_em_mstep, if a component collapses (near-singular U/V), the code continues silently and contaminates all subsequent log-likelihoods.

Fix: Add a checked safeguard after M-step: if any eigenvalue of U or V is below a threshold times the maximum eigenvalue, merge that component into the nearest surviving one and decrement the effective count.

E. Add BIC corrections for the noise component
R/print_summary.R:73 computes BIC as if there were no noise component's extra mixing parameter. Add a noise-penalized BIC term:

BIC_noisy = -2*LL + log(n) * (g * rp + 2*g*(r*(r+1)/2 + p*(p+1)/2) + (g-1))
3. Research Directions
Goal	Suggested approach
Faster EM for high dim	Stochastic/mini-batch EM (Liang et al.) or Variational inference (Blei et al.)
Joint estimation of g and k	Bayesian nonparametrics: Dirichlet Process / Pitman-Yor mixture with Jordan-Zhao noise model
Robust to heavy-tailed matrices	Replace Gaussian components with matrix-variate t-distributions or use MCD noise levels
Better ρ than KS	Use a modified BIC gap (k vs. k-1), or a ratio of marginal likelihoods, over the heuristic KS curve
Immediate priorities
Vectorize E/M-step (R/matrix_utils.R) — this is the single largest speed gain.
Add Ledoit-Wolf shrinkage to mv_em_mstep and mv_compute_init_params — directly lifts clustering accuracy.
Replace hand-rolled DBSCAN with dbscan::dbscan.
Penalized BIC for g selection so users are not guessing.
